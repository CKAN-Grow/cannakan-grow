**Capability:** 2A — Session Conditions

## 1. Purpose

This supplemental Composition Specification defines how Products consume
canonical Growing commencement and preserve unresolved legacy chronology.

It composes committed Foundation authority into deterministic Product meaning.
It does not establish canonical chronology, lifecycle authority, persistence,
implementation, or presentation behavior.

## 3. Composition Decision

Products consume canonical Growing commencement only as supplied by Session
Lifecycle.

```text
Canonical Session Lifecycle
        supplies
Canonical Growing Commencement
        consumed by
Product Composition
        delivered through
Presentation
```

Product Composition may organize and explain canonical commencement. It may
not own, establish, derive, repair, normalize, reinterpret, or persist it.

## 4. Canonical Growing Commencement Composition

When Session Lifecycle supplies authoritative canonical Growing commencement,
Product Composition may:

- make that chronology understandable;
- organize or compare it with other authoritative chronology;
- compose deterministic Product meaning from it; and
- provide it to authorized read-only Product consumers.

Product Composition must consume the canonical fact exactly as supplied.

Growing commencement is never established from Product or presentation state,
Germination completion, Growing Phase creation or updates, first Growing
evidence, another capability record, a projection, migration output,
administrator selection, or inferred, estimated, reconstructed, or approximate
chronology.

Session creation time is authoritative only through the direct-Growing domain
action defined by FN-006. Products may not generalize that bounded rule.

## 5. Chronology Consumption

Products may display canonical meaning through later Presentation, compare and
organize authoritative chronological facts, and compose deterministic behavior
from canonical chronology.

Products may not create, derive, repair, redefine, replace, or select a
substitute for canonical chronology. Circumstantial chronology cannot become
canonical truth through composition.

## 6. Per-Session Consumption

Product behavior depends on whether Session Lifecycle supplies authoritative
Growing commencement for the canonical Session.

Where authoritative commencement is supplied, Products consume it.

Where authoritative commencement is not supplied and Foundation authority
identifies chronology as unresolved, Products preserve that unresolved
meaning.

This boundary governs Product consumption only. It does not determine whether
commencement exists, create a persisted Product status, or establish a new
lifecycle classification.

Foundation governs existence and authority. Implementation may later realize
approved persistence. Product Composition owns neither.

## 7. Legacy Session Composition

A legacy Session may have canonical lifecycle state without authoritative
Growing commencement chronology.

Product Composition must:

- preserve unresolved chronology as unresolved;
- distinguish it from authoritative chronology;
- avoid presenting circumstantial chronology as canonical;
- avoid fabricating chronological precision;
- avoid changing lifecycle state to conceal missing chronology; and
- remain deterministic for equivalent canonical inputs.

Unresolved chronology is a Product composition outcome inherited from the
absence identified by Foundation authority. It is not a lifecycle phase,
lifecycle state, Session status, chronology source, approximate timestamp,
correction, or recovery decision.

This specification composes no migration, chronology recovery, administrator
decision, fallback, estimation, or backdating behavior.

## 8. Entry Path Composition

Product Composition preserves both authorized Growing-entry paths.

### 8.1 Seed-to-Growing

1. Germination completes and remains durable canonical history.
2. A separate authorized Begin Growing transition occurs.
3. Session Lifecycle makes Growing current and supplies canonical Growing
   commencement as one coherent lifecycle outcome.
4. Products consume that commencement without deriving it from Germination
   completion or later activity.

### 8.2 Direct-Growing

1. Authorized direct-Growing Session creation and Growing entry form one
   Foundation-defined domain action.
2. Session Lifecycle supplies canonical Growing commencement when that action
   succeeds.
3. Products consume that commencement without treating generic Session
   creation as universally authoritative.

The paths share lifecycle ownership without becoming the same entry action.

## 9. Session Conditions Composition

Under CS-SC-001A, Grow Method and Environment Type use period applicability
beginning with canonical Growing commencement.

Session Conditions consume commencement supplied by Session Lifecycle. They do
not establish the boundary independently, derive it from legacy or
circumstantial timestamps, infer it from Growing-owned values, repair
unresolved chronology, or acquire lifecycle authority.

The Canonical Platform remains responsible for Current Conditions derivation.
Product Composition consumes the canonical projection and does not derive it.
When required chronology is unresolved, Product Composition preserves the
canonical unresolved result and does not manufacture period applicability.

## 10. Downstream Consumer Composition

Authorized downstream Product consumers may include Session Conditions,
Timeline composition, reporting composition, and future separately authorized
Product capabilities.

Consumers may organize, compare, or explain authoritative commencement within
their approved responsibility. They remain read-only and may not create or
correct commencement, become a lifecycle authority, persist a competing fact,
substitute another timestamp, infer missing chronology, or overwrite canonical
truth.

This specification does not authorize Timeline rendering, reporting or
analytics implementation, Calendar behavior, notifications, or a new consumer
capability.

## 11. Presentation Boundary

Presentation may communicate Product meaning produced from authoritative or
unresolved chronology.

Presentation may not determine whether commencement exists, which timestamp is
authoritative, whether unresolved chronology should be replaced, whether a
fallback should be used, or whether lifecycle state should change.

This specification defines no interface, component, layout, workflow,
interaction, copy, or visual treatment.

## 12. Deterministic Composition

Given the same canonical Session identity, lifecycle path, lifecycle state,
authoritative commencement when supplied, and unresolved meaning when supplied
by Foundation authority, Product Composition must produce the same meaning.

Composition must not depend on retrieval order, navigation history, viewed
phase, render order, component state, cached labels, Product defaults, or
Presentation behavior.

Insufficient canonical chronology remains unresolved rather than guessed.

## 13. Composition Invariants

### INV-CS-SC-001B-01 — Lifecycle ownership

Session Lifecycle exclusively owns canonical Growing commencement.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-02 — Consumption only

Products consume commencement and never establish, derive, repair, normalize,
or reinterpret it.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-03 — FN-007 transition authority

The authorized Begin Growing transition governs Seed-to-Growing entry.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-04 — Distinct entry paths

Seed-to-Growing and direct-Growing retain their distinct Foundation semantics.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-05 — Honest unresolved chronology

Missing authoritative legacy chronology remains unresolved.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-06 — No new lifecycle state

Unresolved chronology creates no lifecycle phase, lifecycle state, or Session
status.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-07 — No substitute timestamp

Circumstantial, inferred, estimated, reconstructed, approximate, or
administrator-selected chronology never becomes canonical commencement.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-08 — Session Conditions remain consumers

Session Conditions may use canonical commencement as an applicability boundary
but acquire no lifecycle authority.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-09 — Current Conditions remain canonical

The Canonical Platform derives Current Conditions; Products and Presentation
remain non-authoritative consumers.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-10 — Downstream consumers remain read-only

Timeline, reporting, and future Product consumers acquire no commencement
ownership.

### INV-CS-SC-001B-11 — Replaceability

Products and Presentation may be replaced without changing canonical
commencement or Product meaning.

## 14. Architectural Boundaries

This specification does not authorize or define:

- Foundation truth or lifecycle and commencement authority;
- lifecycle phases, states, transitions, or corrections;
- implementation behavior or execution;
- persistence, storage, schemas, databases, or migrations;
- APIs, RPCs, triggers, services, or adapters;
- chronology recovery, approximation, backdating, or fallback selection;
- administrator tooling or decisions;
- interfaces, workflows, navigation, or presentation design;
- Timeline or Calendar rendering;
- analytics or reporting implementation;
- notification behavior;
- artificial intelligence or sensors; or
- a broader lifecycle-event or chronology system.

## 15. Acceptance Conditions

This specification is complete only when:

1. Growing commencement is always consumed from Session Lifecycle;
2. Product Composition never derives or repairs commencement;
3. authoritative and unresolved chronology remain distinguishable;
4. unresolved chronology creates no new lifecycle state;
5. both entry paths retain their distinct Foundation semantics;
6. Session Conditions acquire no lifecycle authority;
7. Current Conditions remain Canonical Platform-derived;
8. downstream Product consumers remain read-only;
9. no migration, recovery, persistence, implementation, or UI authority is
   introduced; and
10. governing Foundation and Composition authority remains unchanged.

## 16. Result

This specification composes canonical Growing commencement and unresolved
legacy chronology into deterministic Product meaning while preserving
lifecycle authority, honest uncertainty, distinct entry semantics, read-only
consumption, and Product and Presentation replaceability.

This specification does not authorize implementation. Any implementation
authority must be separately approved through the governing Implementation
Contract workflow.
