## 1. Purpose

This bounded supplemental Composition Specification reconciles the approved
Current Conditions Product meaning with the existing Session Conditions
architecture.

It establishes contextual access; one-or-two-dimension change composition;
canonical no-change and effective-now meaning; correction provenance and time
meaning; Condition History presentation; and one bounded forward-only
declaration for unresolved legacy Sessions.

This specification supplements CS-SC-001, CS-SC-001A, and CS-SC-001B. It
preserves their compatible authority and introduces only the bounded
unresolved-legacy rule in Sections 20 and 21.

This document defines Product Composition only. It does not amend
[IC-SC-001](../../foundation/implementation-contracts/IC-SC-001-session-conditions.md),
authorize implementation, or establish implementation readiness.

## 3. Problem Being Reconciled

Existing authority establishes canonical ownership, period applicability,
Platform-derived Current Conditions, honest unresolved legacy chronology, and
read-only Grow Companion consumption. It does not fully establish:

- one confirmation changing one or both independent dimensions;
- genuinely changed-set and canonical no-change meaning;
- all-or-nothing Product commitment for two changed dimensions;
- successful-operation effective-now meaning;
- immutable original effective time and separate correction time;
- optional correction-note provenance and post-success Corrected presentation;
- contextual `View Conditions` access; or
- present-and-forward truth for an unresolved legacy Session whose earlier
  chronology must remain unavailable.

These Product decisions must precede any later bounded contract amendment.
Implementation precedent cannot supply them.

## 4. Bounded Composition Scope

This specification governs only:

1. Grow Method and Environment Type;
2. Current Conditions and authorized Condition History;
3. concise read-only composition in Active Growing Overview;
4. contextual `View Conditions` routing;
5. Change Conditions for one or both genuinely changed dimensions;
6. no-change and forward-only successful-boundary meaning;
7. correction of one existing assertion;
8. optional correction-note provenance and Corrected presentation;
9. authoritative timestamp presentation; and
10. declaration of both dimensions from a successful boundary forward for an
    unresolved legacy Session.

The last item is the only rule that differs from normal initial-period
composition. It applies only when canonical Growing commencement is
unavailable under Foundation authority and must remain unresolved.

## 5. Canonical Ownership Boundary

The canonical Session owns its Session Conditions. The Canonical Platform
retains their meaning, normalization, applicability, provenance, correction
history, and deterministic Current Conditions projection.

Product Composition organizes approved meaning and routes owning operations.
It does not own condition truth, create a second capability or record model,
persist Current Conditions, establish lifecycle chronology, turn proposals
into truth before success, or make Presentation comparison authoritative.

Presentation may render the composition and collect proposed input. It owns
neither condition evidence nor operation outcomes.

## 6. Composition Exclusions

This specification introduces no:

- additional dimension or vocabulary;
- measurement, target, recommendation, guidance, or automation system;
- per-entry, per-position, per-plant, or arbitrary-record condition model;
- population mutation, Growing-progress or stage meaning, Growing completion,
  or Reflection behavior;
- lifecycle transition, commencement recovery, chronology reconstruction, or
  Germination reconstruction;
- second Current Conditions capability or Product-owned condition history;
- Task, Event, notification, Recent Activity, Session Note, Photo, Document,
  or shared Chronology participation; or
- implementation authority.

Section 27 supplies the complete implementation-mechanics exclusions.

## 7. Authorized Dimensions and Session Applicability

Exactly two dimensions remain authorized:

1. **Grow Method**
2. **Environment Type**

Both describe conditions for the canonical Session as a whole. This scope does
not convert them to CS-SC-001's static Session-wide applicability type.
CS-SC-001A period applicability continues to govern, with independently
attributable histories inside Session chronology.

The dimensions are not scoped per Seed Entry, KAN partition, Cube, Plug,
Planting position, plant, Task, Event, Note, Photo, Document, or arbitrary
record. Each retains its own identity, vocabulary, provenance, and history.
The already governed vocabularies remain unchanged.

## 8. Current Conditions Composition

Current Conditions remain a deterministic canonical projection derived from
condition evidence, not a separately persisted record.

They mean the Grow Method and Environment Type applicable now. The deeper
Current Conditions capability and Active Growing Overview consume the same
projection. Neither owns, copies, renormalizes, or independently derives it.

Equivalent canonical inputs produce equivalent meaning. Missing, unavailable,
and unresolved truth must not be replaced with Product or Presentation
assumptions.

## 9. Contextual Access Composition

Active Growing Overview may present a concise, read-only Current Conditions
composition. The approved contextual route is:

> View Conditions

It opens the deeper Current Conditions capability. It is routing through the
existing Grow Companion composition, not a new canonical operation.

No permanent Conditions destination is added to Grow Companion local
navigation. Contextual access does not duplicate truth, create another
capability or record model, move ownership into Active Growing Overview, or
give the overview mutation authority.

## 10. Change Conditions Composition

Change Conditions records a genuine real-world transition. One flow may
propose a new Grow Method only, a new Environment Type only, or both.

Selections remain proposed and non-canonical during opening, selection,
review, and confirmation presentation. Only successful completion of the
owning canonical operation establishes changed truth.

Change Conditions is distinct from correction. It cannot repair inaccurate
historical evidence, rewrite prior truth, change lifecycle state, or alter
canonical Growing commencement.

## 11. Changed-Set and No-Change Semantics

Before confirmation, the Product determines the genuinely changed set by
comparing each proposed normalized canonical meaning with its Current value.
Only a differing dimension participates.

An unchanged dimension continues without interruption, receives no new
history or duplicate applicability evidence, and is not represented as
changed.

When no proposed dimension differs, the governed meaning is:

> No changes to save

No confirmation action is available; no canonical operation, applicability
evidence, period, history entry, or correction is created; and Current
Conditions remain unchanged.

Equality and no-change rejection cannot depend only on client-side or
Presentation comparison. A later contract must preserve this meaning at the
owning canonical boundary.

## 12. One-or-Two-Dimension Confirmation Composition

One grower confirmation represents one Product operation over the complete
genuinely changed set.

For one changed dimension, only it participates. For two changed dimensions:

- both share one successful effective boundary;
- each retains independent evidence identity and history;
- both become established together; and
- if the complete operation fails, neither becomes established.

This all-or-nothing Product commitment does not merge dimension identity,
vocabulary, or history. It defines no transaction, RPC, lock, rollback,
persistence, or concurrency mechanism.

## 13. Effective-Now Meaning

A normal Change Conditions operation is forward-only. The approved copy is:

> Effective now

Its meaning is:

> The confirmed change begins applying only after the Change Conditions
> operation succeeds.

Opening the flow, selecting a dimension or value, opening Review changes,
opening confirmation, an unsuccessful attempt, or leaving an unfinished flow
establishes neither Current Conditions nor an effective boundary.

No backdating or future scheduling is authorized. The caller selects no past
or future effective boundary. This section defines no clock capture, storage,
transaction, or persistence mechanics.

## 14. Change and Correction Composition

**Change Conditions** records a genuine transition. **Correct a Record** fixes
inaccurate evidence about exactly one existing condition assertion.

A correction creates no real-world transition or new `Effective now` change.
It does not restart Growing, change canonical Growing commencement, change
Session origin, change the initial Growing population, or create lifecycle or
Germination history.

Correction preserves attributable provenance and cannot disguise a real
operational transition or rewrite unrelated evidence.

## 15. Effective-Time and Correction-Time Distinction

Correction preserves the targeted assertion's original effective time. It
does not move, replace, recreate, or convert correction time into that
effective time.

Correction has a separate correction time. If the corrected assertion governs
the Current Conditions projection, the corrected value may become Current
after success. If the assertion is historical and does not govern Current
Conditions, the Current projection remains unchanged.

## 16. Optional Correction-Note Composition

A correction may offer:

> Add a correction note — optional

The note is never required, belongs only to correction provenance, appears
only when supplied, stays behind progressive disclosure or detailed review,
and does not alter condition meaning.

It creates or participates in no Session Note, Task, Event, notification,
Recent Activity, shared Chronology, Photo, Document, or lifecycle state. No
field or storage implementation is defined here.

## 17. Corrected Presentation Composition

`Corrected` is derived post-success presentation over canonical correction
provenance. Before success, the approved copy is:

> This record will be marked Corrected.

Correction must not appear complete before success. After success, a
restrained `Corrected` marker may appear in normal Condition History while
detailed evidence remains behind progressive disclosure.

`Corrected` is not a canonical condition value, lifecycle state, or
applicability state and does not replace correction provenance.

## 18. Normal Commenced-Session Applicability

When authoritative canonical Growing commencement exists, initial Grow Method
and initial Environment Type applicability begin at that commencement.
CS-SC-001A remains authoritative.

The first periods do not move to later Product interaction. The unresolved-
legacy rule neither weakens nor generalizes an exception to this normal rule.

## 19. Normal Operational History

After normal initial applicability exists, successful Change Conditions begins
a new forward-only period for each genuinely changed dimension at the shared
successful boundary.

Earlier periods remain historically true. An unchanged dimension continues
without interruption or duplicate history. History states when each new value
began applying and never makes a later Current value appear earlier.

## 20. Unresolved Legacy Forward-Declaration Composition

When canonical Growing commencement is unavailable and must remain unresolved,
the Product may offer:

> Set Current Conditions

This bounded extension applies only to an unresolved legacy Session. It is not
an initial condition declaration or a Growing commencement declaration.

A successful declaration requires both Grow Method and Environment Type. Both
become authoritative only from the successful declaration boundary forward,
share that boundary, and retain independent evidence identities and histories.

The declaration does not create or alter commencement, resolve missing
commencement, reconstruct lifecycle chronology or earlier applicability,
backfill history, imply pre-success truth, create Germination history, change
Session origin, restart Growing elapsed time, or create a lifecycle
transition.

It permits applicability later than Growing commencement only because
authoritative commencement is unavailable in this bounded legacy case. It is
not an exception to lifecycle authority.

## 21. Earlier Legacy Unavailability

Both truths must remain explicit:

1. conditions before the successful declaration boundary are unavailable;
2. declared conditions from that boundary forward are authoritative.

The earlier interval is not a known, null-valued, inferred, or initial
condition period and is not conditions established when Growing began.

Earlier conditions cannot be reconstructed, inferred, backdated, attached to
commencement, or derived from Session creation, timestamps, Germination,
Tasks, Events, Notes, Photos, Documents, or unrelated records.

Condition History may use:

- `Current Conditions set`;
- `Set from this point forward`; and
- `Earlier conditions unavailable`.

It must not use this copy for a forward-only legacy declaration:

> Established when Growing began

## 22. Condition History and Timestamp Presentation

Condition History presents authoritative absolute date and time in the
grower's governed display timezone.

Session Conditions does not own timezone truth. It consumes the authoritative
display-timezone preference from the appropriate governing capability without
defining that capability.

Effective time, declaration time, and correction time retain separate
meanings. Normal changes display when the value began applying. Legacy
declarations display when Current Conditions were successfully established.
Corrections display original effective time and separate correction time.

Relative-only time is not authoritative. Formatting and timezone conversion
cannot alter the canonical stored instant. If authoritative display timezone
is unavailable, later implementation authority must present honestly without
changing that instant.

No storage type, timezone or locale library, conversion code, fallback
timezone, or schema mechanism is defined here.

### 23.3 CS-SC-001B

## 24. Lifecycle and Grow Companion Boundaries

FN-006 assigns Growing commencement exclusively to Session Lifecycle. Current
Conditions operations cannot establish, alter, replace, infer, repair, or
reconstruct it.

FN-007 governs intentional atomic Begin Growing. `Set Current Conditions` is
not Begin Growing, does not make Growing current, and establishes no
commencement. Completed Germination remains durable and unaffected.
Direct-origin Sessions receive no fabricated Germination truth.

Growing elapsed time derives only from canonical commencement. Condition
operations do not change Session lifecycle chronology.

Under CS-GC-008, Active Growing Overview remains read-only; `View Conditions`
is contextual routing; no duplicate condition capability or record model is
created; and this design adds no permanent Conditions navigation destination.
