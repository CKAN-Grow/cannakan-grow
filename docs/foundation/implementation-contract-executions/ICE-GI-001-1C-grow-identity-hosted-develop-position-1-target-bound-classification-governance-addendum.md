The classification unit is a governed evidence assertion, not an entire
physical row and not every physical column independently.

For every frozen `public.public_member_profiles` owner row, the standard
classification universe contains:

1. one canonical owner-association unit;
2. eighteen canonical value-claim units:

   - `display_name`;
   - `username`;
   - `profile_image`;
   - `cover_image`;
   - `bio`;

   - `favorite_breeders`;
   - `favorite_sources`;
   - `languages`;
   - `city`;
   - `state_province`; and
   - `country`;

3. one combined system profile-type unit covering `profile_type` and
   `account_type`;
4. ten privacy and preference units:

   - `profile_visibility`;
   - `grow_network_discoverable`;
   - `connection_request_permission`;
   - invitation preference `testing_programs`;
   - invitation preference `grow_alongs`;
   - invitation preference `collaborations`;
   - invitation preference `vault_sharing`;
   - invitation preference `breeder_source_opportunities`;
   - `personalization_consent`; and
   - `timezone`;

5. twenty-three field-visibility units, one for every committed
   `grow_identity_field_keys_v1()` key; and
6. twenty-three provenance units, one for every governed field key.

A missing field-visibility row remains an explicit absence-claim unit. Missing
provenance remains a provenance unit.

The standard universe is exactly 76 units per canonical profile row.

Additional anomaly units are generated for:

- orphan visibility records; and
- unauthorized extra provenance keys.

Duplicate, null, malformed, or unstable canonical owner keys stop universe
freezing.

One physical source row may produce multiple units. One semantic claim may not
produce multiple competing units. Multiple protected fields in one source row
are separate units only when they represent separate governed claims.

Arrays are one field claim rather than one unit per element.

The `username` unit consolidates:

- `public_member_profiles.username`;
- `public_handle`; and
- permitted private-account corroboration.

The `country` unit consolidates:

- `country`;
- `country_code`; and
- attributable legacy-location evidence.

A view or derived output is not categorized directly. It is traced to its base
classification units and separately verified against its governed projection
contract.

Recognition, Vault, and Network source records are not category units.
Relationships involving those domains remain source-domain verification
surfaces. This prevents Position 1 from acquiring authority owned by those
domains.

## 7. Exact Universe-Freezing Rule

A later separately authorized inspection must:

1. authenticate the exact hosted, persistent, non-default Supabase `develop`
   target and conclusively exclude production;
2. bind every operation explicitly to the authenticated internal target
   identity, without implicit linkage or defaults;
3. establish an approved read-only database role and session;
4. begin one `REPEATABLE READ, READ ONLY` transaction;
5. verify within the transaction that the session and transaction are
   read-only;
6. verify the migration ledger, expected schema, relation identities, key
   constraints, field-key contract, and relevant catalog definitions;
7. capture snapshot attribution containing:

   - authenticated target identity reference;
   - observation time;
   - PostgreSQL snapshot identifier;
   - migration-ledger digest; and
   - schema-signature digest;

8. enumerate canonical profile anchors in deterministic `user_id` order while
   retaining raw identifiers only transiently;
9. generate the 76 standard units for each canonical anchor;
10. enumerate orphan visibility rows and unauthorized provenance keys as
    anomaly units;
11. reconcile source-row counts, expected units, anomaly units, and stable
    identifier uniqueness;
12. freeze the complete classification manifest and its digest before making
    any category decision;
13. use only that immutable manifest and the same consistent evidence snapshot
    for classification; and
14. close the transaction with `ROLLBACK`, including on success.

Rows created, changed, or deleted after the database snapshot are outside that
classification instance.

The universe formula is:

`frozen total = 76 × P + orphan-visibility units + unauthorized-provenance-key units`

where `P` is the frozen count of canonical profile anchors.

Required per-surface subtotals must also be frozen.

If the snapshot, schema, field-key contract, stable keys, manifest, counts,
subtotals, or reconciliation is inconsistent, classification must not begin.

If later treatment is proposed, a new separately authorized drift check must
prove that the target still corresponds to the classified snapshot.

## 8. Stable Identifier Design

Each classification unit has:

- a snapshot-independent pseudonymous `unit_key`; and
- a snapshot-bound `classification_id`.

The canonical forms are:

`P1C1/<target-ref>/<unit-kind>/<owner-ref>/<claim-key>`

and:

`<unit-key>/<snapshot-ref>`

Definitions:

- `target-ref` is a digest of the authenticated branch-project identity and
  fixed target role;
- `unit-kind` identifies owner association, value claim, profile type,
  privacy or preference, field visibility, provenance, or anomaly;
- `owner-ref` is a SHA-256 digest of the target-bound canonical UUID locator;
- `claim-key` is a fixed non-sensitive vocabulary item; and
- `snapshot-ref` is a digest of the database snapshot, schema signature,
  migration-ledger signature, and observation boundary.

Raw UUIDs, usernames, profile values, emails, personal data, and hosted project
identifiers must not appear in procedural classification identifiers.

Hashing supports integrity and pseudonymous correlation only. It does not prove
truth, provenance, ownership, or causality.

Null values and absent field-visibility or provenance records use the anchored
owner and claim key.

Duplicate identifier output, a missing anchor, a malformed primary key, a
relation without an approved stable key, or inability to reproduce the
canonical locator stops classification.

The following are prohibited as stable identifiers:

- `ctid`;
- row order;
- display name;
- timestamp alone; and
- any mutable Presentation identifier.

## 11. Deterministic Category Tests and Precedence

| Category | Exact decision test | Disqualifying condition | Adopted later treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SUPPORTED` | The snapshot fact is complete; the value or absence is permitted by adopted authority; semantic and referential checks pass; positive permitted attribution establishes the claim; and no fabricated cause or unresolved conflict exists. | Missing attribution for a present value, conflict, prohibited source, proven fabricated cause, or invalid structure or reference | Retain the supported value or truthful absence. |
| `FABRICATED` | Positive authenticated causal evidence proves the current assertion was introduced by the Position 1 default, backfill, trigger, client fallback, or equivalent evidence-free completion and was not later validly confirmed. | Literal equality alone, migration presence alone, timestamp alone, or missing causal chain | Make the fabricated assertion absent through a later separately authorized treatment. |
| `INVALID` | Complete authenticated evidence conclusively proves that a current assertion violates an adopted vocabulary, required owner or reference relationship, protected-domain boundary, uniqueness rule, or structural requirement. | Unavailable record, missing provenance alone, merely suspicious value, or unresolved contradictory evidence | Exclude the assertion from canonical output. Any correction requires separate authority. |
| `AMBIGUOUS` | Permitted evidence is absent, incomplete, conflicting, provenance-free, unauthenticated, insufficient for causality, or otherwise cannot conclusively satisfy another category test. | None; this is the fail-closed residual category. | Preserve raw evidence, quarantine it from canonical output, and grant no broader privacy or meaning. |

Overlap and precedence rules are:

1. A semantically valid but unattributable present value is `AMBIGUOUS`, not
   `SUPPORTED`.
2. Equality with `grower`, `new`, or another migration fallback is
   `AMBIGUOUS` unless positive permitted evidence proves fallback causality.
3. Missing provenance is not fabrication.
4. Proven fabrication takes precedence over invalidity when the fabrication
   cause is conclusive. Additional invalidity is recorded only as a secondary
   finding.
5. Conclusive invalidity applies before support.
6. Conflicting conclusive evidence becomes `AMBIGUOUS`; precedence must not
   manufacture certainty.
7. A value conclusively known to violate authority is `INVALID`.
8. A value whose truth cannot be determined is `AMBIGUOUS`.
9. A required owner or reference relationship conclusively absent in a
   complete snapshot is `INVALID`.
10. An optional permitted relationship conclusively absent in the snapshot is
    a `SUPPORTED` absence.
11. A permitted value conclusively absent in the complete snapshot is
    `SUPPORTED` as truthful absence.
12. An unavailable record is not an absence. It yields `AMBIGUOUS` or a
    completeness stop.
13. When approved rules cannot resolve apparent overlap conclusively, the unit
    is `AMBIGUOUS`.
14. Every unit receives exactly one primary category.
